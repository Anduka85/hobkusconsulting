Who we are

Built From the Ground Up, Like the Operations We Deliver

HOBKUS was founded with a clear conviction: the gap between a transport business idea and a functioning operation is not a strategy problem — it is an implementation problem.

Our people come from the transport industry, bringing over 15 years of hands-on experience in recruitment, operational management, and transport coordination. We understand the realities of building and running a transport company because we have worked within the industry ourselves.

Most consulting firms stop at the plan. We stay through execution.

From establishing the first legal entity to putting the first truck on the road, we coordinate every essential function — company structure, recruitment, fleet, operations, processes, and commercial development — under one integrated implementation roadmap.

Our focus is Romania. We understand the regulatory environment, the labour market, the carrier ecosystem, and the commercial landscape. Combined with international operational standards and practical industry experience, this local expertise makes HOBKUS the right partner for businesses entering or expanding in the Romanian transport market.
